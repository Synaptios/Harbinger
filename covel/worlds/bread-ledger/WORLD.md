# The Bread Ledger

London during Mordred’s succession crisis in Book XXI of Thomas Malory’s Le Morte Darthur. The guild requires Alice’s delivery ledger before dusk. The clerk decides whether to disclose the names, accompany Alice to the river, or look for other evidence.

The Thames landing has one guild storehouse slot. Its footpath leads only to the landing. The storehouse sign and porter’s name come from the episode’s approved lists, and both persist after discovery. The porter keeps the guild tally and knows the local sack-marking routine. The clerk can learn the routine by asking the porter.
